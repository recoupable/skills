# Visual-story dry-run review

A fresh-context reviewer used the revised skill on two synthetic cases from evals.json: an invoice/purchase-order comparison carousel with an existing orange cover and no supporting artwork, and a copy-only repair with suitable existing illustrations. No providers, generation, spending, publishing or customer data were involved.

The first plan identified AI in the hook, named supplied documents, returned source-linked mismatches, reserved approval for a person, and offered the free skill separately from paid client usage. It reused the orange cover artwork and planned distinct simple supporting scenes. The second plan replaced generic headings with an explicit agent narrative, retained suitable imagery, and refreshed dependent exports/review without scheduling changes. Both included headline-only and caption-hidden review and treated three-second comprehension as a target.

The reviewer identified a conflict between the thumbnail generation step and cover reuse. The author corrected the main step to reuse suitable approved art and generate only when needed. No other material instruction gaps were found. This is qualitative planning evidence, not a live rendering test, a timed reader study or a measured improvement over a baseline. The user-reviewed carousel that prompted the revision remains in the private content workspace, not this public skill.
