# Normalize campaign collections

Set one contract for company explanations and another for founder commentary. Keep the task and
reader decision easy to find, use active concrete clauses, and stabilize terms for the same concept.
Differences in length should reflect meaningful evidence or exceptions, not arbitrary variety.

Review all openings together. A reader should distinguish the jobs without seeing the artwork.
Check input, documented action and inspectable output against the source. Preserve IDs, sequence,
figures, dates, links, negation and uncertainty. Do not convert a plan into completion, an absent
measurement into zero, or an instruction file into proof of a hosted capability.

Then review founder takes together: each adds a decision or review criterion, not a generic approval
of AI. Keep first-person hypotheses honest and avoid repeating the same “I’d want” construction in
most entries. Confirm one truthful website invitation in every variant. Normalize only the approved
plain-text payload; save a hash and preserve real paragraph newlines.

These principles adapt the collection review in recoup-internal-content-normalize-text. The complete
procedure above is local to this skill and does not require another installation.
