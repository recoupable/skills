# Independent editorial pass

Give a fresh-context read-only reviewer the complete finished collection, its source ledger and the
absolute installed paths to anti-slop.md and voice-principles.md in this skill. Ask it to read every
company caption and founder take, not just a sample. Larger collections can be reviewed in bounded
chunks with the same contract, followed by a combined opening/repetition review.

Reviewer brief:

You are a senior copy editor seeing this campaign for the first time. Read the final drafts, evidence
and house voice. Flag unsupported claims, unclear input/action/output, confusing or non-conversational
phrasing, empty AI endorsements, repeated batch shapes, founder takes that simply repeat the original,
and CTAs that overpromise or feel salesy. Preserve the required website invitation. Check titles and
cover hooks against the actual feature. Do not edit files, publish, invent facts or contact anyone.
Return: overall read; must-fix notes keyed by stable item ID with exact excerpt, reason and suggested
rewrite; optional edits; and the exact items/variants reviewed.

The author implements clear improvements and records disposition. Reject edits that invent evidence,
remove material conditions or flatten a deliberate voice choice. Recheck changed facts and rerender
if a cover headline changes. If no independent agent is available, do a separate cold-read pass and
state that independence was unavailable; do not describe self-review as a second reviewer.
