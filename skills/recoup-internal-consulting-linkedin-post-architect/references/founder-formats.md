# Founder formats — the 6 repeatable structures (+ entry-price selection)

Use these structures with the workspace author's own verified material. All example fragments
below are **fictional writing exercises**, not business history or publishable claims.

## Raw material first (the flop diagnosis)

When a proven format flops, the first diagnosis is **empty inventory, not broken format** — the
writer had nothing of their own to put inside it. Before structuring any post, confirm the raw
material exists and is verified:

| Category | What it is | Where it already lives in this workspace |
|---|---|---|
| **Numbers** | Any real figure + its context. Size doesn't matter; the story around it does ("$180 MRR after 9 months of nights-and-weekends" beats a bare "$180 MRR"). | `business/metrics/`, `proof/case-studies/`, verified client outcomes. **Evidence discipline applies — verified numbers only.** |
| **Scars** | Failures, wrong calls, embarrassing lessons. Least-supplied, most-trusted content: readers know nobody exaggerates to look worse. | Publishable lessons explicitly selected from the workspace; verify their sources and sharing scope |
| **Build log** | Whatever is being built right now — progress is watchable before results exist. | git history, the nightly demand-engine runs, `products/`, skills being authored |

`signals/` is the practice's inventory reservoir; these three categories are the checklist for the
**personal/practice material** that call-mining doesn't capture. If a category is thin, that's the
gap to fill before forcing a format.

## Entry price — how to pick the format

Each format costs something. Pick by what's actually in hand today, not by mood:

| Format | Entry price | Can the author run it today? |
|---|---|---|
| Build log | Zero — just be building something | Always (the OS itself is a build log) |
| Documented failure | A story that stings a little to tell | Whenever a scar is logged |
| Value post | Having done the thing recently | After any delivered engagement/workflow |
| The receipt | A win you can screenshot | Only with a verified artifact |
| Contrast hook | A gap between who you are and what you did | When the gap is real |
| Milestone chapter | A story people already watched you tell | Only after months of consistent posting |

Default: **start with the two cheapest formats available** (usually build log + one of failure/value).

## The 6 formats

### 1. The build log
Post the process, not the outcome. The audience for an unfinished thing is small but it's the right
audience — people invest in what they watched being made, and early watchers become early users.
Engagement will look weak by vanity metrics; that's expected and fine.
- Shape: what I'm building → what happened this week → what's stuck/next. No triumph tone.
- **the author:** "The nightly engine drafted its 20th article while I slept. Here's what it still gets wrong." ·
  "I'm teaching an agent to reconcile a CRM. Day 4: it caught a drift I'd missed."

### 2. The documented failure
Give the losing years the wordcount; give the win one line, underexplained. **Specific embarrassing
detail does the heavy lifting** — a concrete low ("the agent quoted the client a number it invented,
and I'd already hit send") beats an abstract one ("it was a hard time"). Undersupplied because
everyone's feed persona is winning; that's exactly why it's trusted.
- Shape: the scar, told plainly → what it cost → one-line turn. No lesson-lecture at the end.
- **the author:** a fictional studio discovers that its demo missed a required input; the next version validates that field before drafting.

### 3. The value post
Teach something done recently enough to remember the steps. The rule that separates it from a
listicle: **every instruction carries its because** ("start with X — because Y"). Advice without
reasoning is a list, and lists don't build trust. Specific beats broad: "how I got a 12-person label
onto agents in a week" beats "how to adopt AI."
- This is the save/share engine (bookmark farmer). Pairs with a "save this" infographic
  (`recoup-internal-consulting-graphics`, `framework-blocks`).
- Maps 1:1 to the **expertise/teaching archetype** in `post-patterns.md` — use STORM's teaching
  variant for the body.

### 4. The receipt
Show, don't describe. A screenshot-able artifact (dashboard, unprompted client DM, a chart moving)
with **at most one line of text — the restraint is the format**. A claim can be typed by anyone; an
artifact had to be earned. Power comes from repetition: the same dashboard posted monthly becomes a
series people follow, not a flex they scroll past.
- **Evidence discipline is the hard gate:** a receipt is posted only when the underlying number is
  verified (contract/Attio/primary source). Never a mocked-up or projected artifact.
- **the author:** monthly practice-dashboard snapshot · a client's before/after cycle-time chart (with
  permission) · an agent's run-log doing something unassisted.

### 5. The contrast hook
Two facts side by side; the gap between them does all the work. **Don't explain, don't celebrate —
the reader resolves the gap themselves**, and resolving it is what makes them share. The classic
shape is [outcome] + [identity that makes the outcome improbable]. A big number alone is not a
contrast; feeds are full of big numbers.
- When writing line 1 for any post, this device lives in `recoup-internal-consulting-linkedin-hooks`
  (pattern 8 — the gap, not the number).
- **the author:** "Cedar Lantern Studio's prototype fits on one page. Its exception log fills a notebook." (fictional)

### 6. The milestone chapter
A receipt proves a win to strangers; a **milestone connects the win to the story followers already
watched**. The move that makes it work: quote/embed your own old post from the beginning of the arc —
the artifact makes the journey un-fakeable, no narration needed. On LinkedIn: reshare-with-thoughts
of your own old post, or a screenshot of it embedded in the new one.
- The only format that can't be run on demand — it's unlocked by having posted consistently. Run the
  other five and this one arrives on its own.
- **the author:** when the nightly engine's first published pillar lands a client, quote the original
  "I'm building a content engine" post.

## How these map onto the 5 archetypes

Not a competing taxonomy — the archetypes (in `post-patterns.md`) say what **job** a post does; the
founder formats say what **raw material + shape** fills it:

| Founder format | Archetype it usually serves |
|---|---|
| Build log, receipt, milestone chapter | Personal story / proof (trust + reach) |
| Documented failure | Personal / vulnerability story |
| Value post | Expertise / teaching |
| Contrast hook | A **hook-level device** usable across archetypes |

Rotation guidance still comes from the archetype layer; use the entry-price table to pick which
format fills today's slot.

## Reuse note

A format that lands once is a series, not a one-off — receipts repeat monthly, build logs weekly,
failures return later as contrast hooks or milestone chapters. The reuse plays (repost winners,
quote your winners, fan an outlier into multiple posts) are owned by **`recoup-internal-consulting-content-recycler`**.
