# Catalog condition report

## Result and scope

State the evidenced condition and the purpose/perimeter reviewed. Distinguish analytical completion from unresolved findings or action readiness. Identify case ID, source cutoff, rights, territories and periods.

## Evidence and coverage

Link the private source index, identity crosswalk, rights schedule and check-results table. Show applicable, inspected, pass, finding, unknown and excluded counts for each check/service. Explain sample selection and limits. State absent/inaccessible evidence explicitly.

## Material findings

For each finding, provide stable ID/cause, affected objects and scope, exact evidence/version, observed issue, supported consequence, uncertainty, priority and required remedy. Do not add overlapping estimates for the same cause. Mark exposure not quantified where unsupported.

## Rights and registration observations

Distinguish documented interests from assertions, registered claims, mandates and software access. State the searched systems/date and the limits of negative public results. Preserve retained interests and separate deal eligibility.

## Assets and documents

Report inspected technical properties and availability separately from owner reports and backup assertions. Name missing authentic documents/assets and the specific dependent conclusion or action.

## Bounded handoffs

List finding ID, next owning stage, exact evidence/action needed, owner and unresolved dependency. No external remediation has occurred merely because this report recommends it.

## Remaining uncertainty

Identify the conclusions that cannot be reached from current evidence, including unchecked services, periods and post-transfer source/target/financial observations.
