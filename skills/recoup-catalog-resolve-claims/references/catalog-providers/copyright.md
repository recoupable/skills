# U.S. Copyright Office operations

Checked 2026-10-22. This profile supports evidence preparation and current procedure
selection, not legal adjudication or autonomous human certification. Other countries
require their own verified procedures; U.S. support is not worldwide registration.

## Initial registration

Identify the exact composition and/or sound recording, authorship, claimant,
publication facts, included/excluded material and authentic deposit. These work types
are distinct. Select the current official individual/group option only after checking
its requirements, rather than assuming an album or one upload makes every claim
eligible. Prepare current form, deposit, fee and human attestation scope before any
authorized filing. [Work types](https://www.copyright.gov/register/pa-sr.html),
[current registration portal](https://www.copyright.gov/registration/).

## Supplementary registration

For an existing registration, identify the original number, the error or amplification,
supporting facts and applicable limited procedure. Supplementary registration adds
linked information; it does not erase or replace the original registration. Preserve
both records and do not treat the process as a generic edit/delete API.
[Circular 8](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ08.pdf).

## Recordation of transfer documents

Recordation is a distinct document operation. Identify the signed instrument,
affected work/title list, parties, scope, dates and current filing requirements.
Review public disclosure and fees. The current recordation site distinguishes
transfer/other documents and termination notices; do not treat a notice of termination
as an ordinary catalog metadata update. [Recordation overview](https://www.copyright.gov/recordation/).

## Result and remaining action

Keep packet readiness, application receipt, correspondence, office decision and
observed record separate. Missing claimant/publication facts or authentic certification
leave that filing unready; continue unrelated catalog work. Do not fabricate dates,
signatures or legal conclusions, or claim a prepared packet is registered. Provider
processing and legal effect cannot be inferred from a successful local validation.
