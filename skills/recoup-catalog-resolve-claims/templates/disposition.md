# Case disposition

Case/provider case ID; as-of date; source versions:

## Requested and granted outcomes

Original requested remedy; provider's actual disposition and rationale; unresolved or denied portions. Provider administrative closure is separate from substantive resolution.

## Rights/record outcome

Accepted change, effective period and actual external readback. Record unchecked/partial/incorrect observations and remaining conflicts.

## Accounting outcome

Reported/proposed/accepted/posted adjustment by currency and period, calculation and provider evidence. Do not describe an unsupported request as an accepted amount.

## Payment outcome

Remittance reference and provider status; separately matched bank receipt/evidence status; fees/FX/allocations; residuals. State unverified where no receipt evidence exists.

## Residual work and continuation

Remaining rights, periods or money questions; exact next evidence/action; owner; authority and source freshness; prior sent records that must not be replayed. Mention a scheduled follow-up only if it was actually requested and created.
