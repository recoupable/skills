# Evidence register and limits

Research checked 2026-10-27. Use these sources as a dated foundation; recheck live platform rules, metric definitions, provider capabilities and pricing before execution. Guidance in the other references combines these sources with explicit craft/engineering hypotheses. It is not a measured Recoup growth result.

## Primary article

[Theo: analysis of 53 AI animation pages](https://x.com/theoxmotion/status/2114173716280070441), published 2026-10-25; article ID `2114165687467786240`.

Reported sample: 53 pages, first 12 displayed reels each, 636 total, collected October 24–25. Discovery started from successful pages; pinned posts were included. The reported median of page-level middle-reel views was 112.8K for two characters, 110K for one, and 30.2K without characters. On the median page the largest sampled reel held 45% of sample views. These are reported snapshots, not reproduced raw data or causal estimates.

The author emphasizes recognizable characters, caption-led formats, repeatable production, follow-ups to hits and timing tests. Four caption families recur: choice, situational recognition, factual explanation and interpersonal sharing. A multi-account team's ownership is inferred from linked bios and aligned schedules. The article's thirty-reel recommendation is a heuristic. Winner selection, pinned old hits and unmatched post ages limit generalization; revenue and failure-base rates are absent.

Retrieval scope: author/title/ID verified with X API; complete textual body retrieved through a public mirror; eleven embedded stills inspected. Five embedded videos were downloaded and visually sampled, not reviewed in continuous audiovisual playback. No independent 53-account audit or reproduction of the reported statistics was performed. The reusable skill contains neither the complete article nor third-party media.

## Platform primary sources

### YouTube Shorts discovery

[Search and discovery tips — Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en-GB).

YouTube describes viewer personalization, choosing to view, average view duration/percentage and satisfaction signals. Topic demand, competing videos and seasonality also affect distribution. It specifies no minimum posting cadence. This supports measuring several viewer behaviors, not declaring a universal posting hour, format advantage or guaranteed retention threshold.

### YouTube metric definitions

[Get started creating Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en) and [Content tab analytics — Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en).

Public view counting changed March 31, 2025 to starts/replays without a minimum watch duration. Engaged views remain available separately; the help page distinguishes them from public views and ties Shorts earnings/eligibility to engaged views. Studio also exposes feed exposure and viewed-versus-swiped behavior. Preserve the native metric names and current definitions in each export.

### YouTube originality and disclosure

[Channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en).

The July 2025 clarification names repetitive/mass-produced material “inauthentic content.” A consistent format is not itself a ban: the substance must remain meaningfully varied and valuable. Do not infer earnings from views or confuse monetization policy with a universal recommendation-suppression rule.

[GenAI disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en).

The current page distinguishes realistic/meaningful synthetic depictions from clearly non-realistic animation and minor assistance; it also addresses generated music. Determine applicability to the actual video and audio before publishing, rather than applying or removing a flag solely because the account uses cartoons.

### Instagram experimentation and originality

[Meta: Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/), December 2024, updated June 2025.

Trials initially distribute to non-followers and expose engagement observations around 24 hours. Sharing with everyone can be manual or automatic based on the first 72 hours. This is actual distribution, not a private preview or controlled randomized test. Verify account availability and provider support; do not infer API support from a native app feature.

[Meta: helping creators find new audiences](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2024/04/ajudando-o-criador-de-conteudo-a-encontrar-novos-publicos/), Portuguese primary announcement, April 2024.

Meta describes favoring original posts over matching reposts in recommendation areas and changes concerning aggregators. This supports preserving original authorship; it does not establish that every reuse of one's own master across platforms is suppressed.

The older Instagram ranking blog did not load during this research. Do not attribute unverified exact signal weights or a 2026 “algorithm formula” to it.

### TikTok recommendations

[TikTok: how recommendations work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content) and [official recommendation explainer](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-we-recommend-videos?lang=en-IE).

TikTok describes interactions, content information and user information as ranking inputs; watching, skipping and completion matter, with weights depending on the surface and user. The current support material was accessible through indexed excerpts; direct retrieval redirected unsuccessfully. The 2020 newsroom explanation is older supporting context, not proof of unchanged present-day weights.

## Provider primary sources

[Runway: Gen-4 Image References](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/40042718905875-Creating-with-Gen-4-Image-References).

The guide documents reference-conditioned characters/environments, composition sketches and individual iteration of complex elements. Reference images guide outputs but do not establish measured consistency for this workflow. Follow the actual endpoint's current input limits rather than carrying limits from this web UI guide into another model.

[Runway: Gen-4 video prompting](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/39789879462419-Gen-4-Video-Prompting-Guide).

The guide recommends a clean input image, simple observable motion, positive descriptions and incremental changes. It warns that several scene changes/actions in a short generation can conflict. These are provider-specific recommendations supporting the shot-first approach; this skill does not require Runway or claim any example creator used it.

## What the evidence does not establish

No guaranteed viral recipe, causal duo advantage, universal thirty-post validation threshold, account-network owner identity, exact competitor generation model, independently reproduced benchmark, or sustainable revenue estimate. Production routes and measurement rules are operational methods for testing those unknowns with the selected account, not a claim that the unknowns have already been solved.
