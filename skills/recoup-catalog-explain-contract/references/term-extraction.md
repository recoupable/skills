# Clause-linked terms and obligations

Read all supplied documents before resolving a term. Inventory original agreement,
repertoire schedules, exhibits, addenda, amendments, execution pages, assignments,
consents, releases and notices independently. Record draft/apparently_executed/unknown;
an electronic-looking signature is an observation, not authenticated legal execution.
An unsigned amendment does not silently replace a supplied executed grant.

For every consequential term, use the template's term record: source/version/page/clause,
party and beneficiary, asset IDs or exact schedule scope, right, territory, dates or
trigger, value/basis/currency/scale, conditions/exclusions and interpretation confidence.
Distinguish a quoted proposition from the practical interpretation. Missing schedules
block the repertoire conclusion; they need not block explaining the payment clause.

Review the grants, exclusions, ownership vs license, term, delivery/release triggers,
options/renewal, notice windows, termination, reversion, collection tail, assignment
and consent. Explain each amendment's affected clauses and effective trigger. Where
precedence is unclear preserve both readings and identify the decision required.
Never calculate expiry from signature if first release/delivery/recoupment is the trigger.

For accounting, extract rate and revenue base together; commission order, cost eligibility,
recoupment pool, cross-collateralization, reserves/release rules, adjustments, reporting
lag, payment thresholds and audit access/notice/limitation periods. Unknown bases remain
unknown. Contractual entitlement is not a reported ledger or a bank receipt.

For operations, capture who must deliver, approve, account, pay, notify or consent;
what event starts the deadline; and what source establishes the event. Keep permissions
for samples, features, AI use, likeness and sync tied to the actual text, music and
parties. Missing permissions are documentary gaps, not permission to invent a release.
Legal validity, remedies, conflicting interpretations and jurisdiction rules need current
authoritative verification and appropriate specialist review, not a summary's certainty.

Output the answer, terms, obligation list, missing-document requests and unresolved
interpretations. Preserve source-derived terms when downstream records disagree;
correcting a database cannot repair an unsigned agreement. Do not send notice, negotiate,
sign, waive an audit right, authorize registration or rewrite a balance in this stage.

Synthetic acceptance review: a three-year term from first commercial release with no
release evidence has no computed expiry; a producer's five percent of defined net
receipts is not five percent composition ownership; a collection tail may survive
termination without extending the ownership grant; a missing schedule leaves asset scope
unknown even when reporting obligations are readable.
