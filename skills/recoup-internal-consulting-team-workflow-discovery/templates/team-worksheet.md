## One recent job

1. Which request did you complete recently, who asked for it, and what triggered it?
2. What did the recipient accept as finished? What did they need to do next?
3. Describe the steps, including handoffs, waiting, checks, and corrections.
4. How often did the job occur in the last complete period? What record supports the count?
5. How much hands-on time did each role spend? Which figures are estimates?
6. Which files and tools were required, and who can approve access?
7. What already helps, who maintains it, and where does it fail?
8. Which mistake would make the output unusable? Who reviews it?
9. What work would receive any recovered time? What business result would matter?
10. What would help or prevent you from trying a revised process?
