# International composition collection

Checked 2026-10-22. Start from right, territory, effective period and existing direct,
reciprocal or subpublisher mandate. Do not recommend joining every society or file
parallel competing claims merely because a territory appears in a usage export.

## PRS / MCPS

Treat performance and mechanical collection scope separately. Prepare work/tunecode,
parties, shares and actual mandates; inspect current member instructions before
selecting new registration or update. Public registration content was dynamic and
not readable in this pass, so full field validation remains unverified. A PRS login
does not, by itself, establish MCPS collection scope. [Registration route](https://www.prsformusic.com/music-and-royalties/register-and-manage-music/register-your-works).

## SOCAN / CMRRA

SOCAN's current portal covers work/catalogue, cue-sheet, agreement and case functions.
Its reproduction-rights guide distinguishes mechanical interests and writer-publisher
links from performance shares. Derive the entered rights from the actual agreement;
do not assume every publishing relationship has identical ownership. [Portal](https://www.socan.com/new-member-portal-features-functionality/),
[reproduction guide](https://www.socan.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Managing-Your-Reproduction-Rights.pdf).

CMRRA documents song registration for publishers and self-published writers. Prepare
the actual current form or eligible receiver-specific CWR process, and verify overlap
with the represented party's existing mandates. The reviewed tutorial page establishes
the route, not a machine-tested field schema. [Registration tutorial](https://cmrra.ca/help-centre/how-to-register-your-songs-video-tutorial/).

## SACEM

Use the actual authorized member account and current declaration instructions. The
legacy detailed guide URL returned 404 during this pass; membership entry alone
does not establish a supported submission format. Prepare repertoire/party/mandate
evidence and unresolved current requirements, explicitly unvalidated for filing.
[Member entry](https://createurs-editeurs.sacem.fr/authentification).

## GEMA

The documented portal workflow selects contract account, contributors, work details
and an overview before submission, then exposes a provisional confirmation. AR/VR
shares use role-specific pools; preserve those semantics and review any automatic
equal allocation rather than treating an empty field as an intended split. Provisional
confirmation and observed repertoire remain separate. [Work declaration](https://www.gema.de/en/w/help/creators/works-repertoire/submit-a-notification-for-your-works/how-register-works).

## APRA AMCOS

The writer portal/app supports work registration, including adding an ISRC later.
Its current guide permits certain edits in the portal but directs title/split changes
to the works team with current/proposed values and appropriate co-writer involvement.
Prepare a WorkID-linked correction instead of a duplicate registration. Preserve
actual publisher mandate and requested scope. [Registration and updates](https://www.apraamcos.com.au/resources/get-paid/register-songs).

For every society retain provider/local IDs alongside the underlying work identity,
authentic acknowledgment, raw item state and exact accepted-record observation.
Account rules, language, eligibility and downstream reciprocal outcomes still need
verification. These are bounded preparation routes, not worldwide autonomous filing.
