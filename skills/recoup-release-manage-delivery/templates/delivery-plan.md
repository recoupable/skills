# Existing-release delivery plan

## Requested mode and scope

Correction, UGC enrollment or migration; case/action IDs; represented party and role; distributor/provider account; existing release editions/recordings; destinations/territories. Record source delivery evidence.

## Before/after and assets

Exact field changes, identifiers, track order and asset hashes; supporting credit/rights/technical evidence; per-destination manifest. Do not silently change unrelated values or recording identity.

## Provider validation and capability

Official rules/check date, exact schema/version, account eligibility, route, required assets, validation, fees/disclosures, irreversible effects and human/provider steps. Distinguish validated preparation from implemented submission/readback.

## Execution and retry plan

Existing explicit scope, exact payload/version, per-item attempt keys and receipt capture. For uncertain attempts inspect current provider state before retrying. Mark actions with missing authority or evidence separately.

## Mode-specific requirements

For UGC: exclusive-rights/territory basis, reference eligibility, existing claims, policy and exclusions. For migration: source and target manifests, verified continuity requirements, target readback, authorized removal sequencing and residual destinations. For corrections: accepted provider values versus each destination's observed values.

## Result and continuation

Per-item preparation/submission/acceptance/observation state, actual receipts/errors, outstanding provider/human steps and next owner. UGC active reference/policy, observed claim and revenue stay separate. Migration completion does not establish financial continuity.
