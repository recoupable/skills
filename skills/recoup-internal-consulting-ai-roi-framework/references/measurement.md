# Measurement rules

## Four different statements

A forecast estimates opportunity. A provisional claim observes an improvement without verified economic capture. A realized claim verifies captured benefit in a specified interval. An annualized run rate extrapolates a rate; it is not cumulative realized benefit. An initiative may have all four at once.

Measure comparable units and representative segments; record sample sizes, dates, work mix, source system, data owner, method and uncertainty. Include human review, exception handling, rework and quality failures in cycle time and cost. Report missing baseline or unavailable source as missing, not zero.

## Departmental evidence

| Function | Start with | Insufficient on its own |
|---|---|---|
| Finance operations | Unit cost, end-to-end turnaround, error/rework rate, captured expense reduction | Survey estimates of hours saved |
| Marketing/sales | Conversion, qualified pipeline, acquisition cost, contribution and attribution | Output count or raw leads |
| Engineering | Ticket-to-production time, comparable delivery throughput, checks passing, escaped defects | PR counts, lines of code, sentiment or AI spend |
| Shared foundation | Comparable deployment cost/time, review duration, reliability/security, use-case launches and verified reuse | Training attendance or infrastructure availability |

Retain the client's actual existing outcome metrics; the table is a starting menu. Pipeline is leading evidence rather than booked revenue, and faster delivery must preserve quality. Use objective records where available and disclose self-report limitations.

## Attribution and financial reconciliation

For a matched treatment/control design, estimate incremental rate lift as (treatment after − treatment before) − (control after − control before). Check comparability, common period, sample sizes, mix changes, pricing/market effects and uncertainty; an arithmetic difference alone does not establish causality. Prefer randomization when practical. When a control is unavailable, explain the alternative method and limitations rather than asserting full causality.

Incremental units = attributable rate lift × eligible exposed volume in the measured period. Incremental revenue = incremental units × realized revenue per unit. Incremental contribution = incremental revenue × applicable contribution margin, with all variable costs included exactly once. Subtract remaining incremental AI costs to obtain net benefit. If the margin already includes some costs, do not subtract them again. Show scenario forecasts for future unexposed volume separately.

For efficiency: realized period net benefit = verified period economic benefit − implementation costs incurred in the selected view − operating/review/rework costs for the same period. ROI ratio = net benefit / included investment costs, when costs are positive and the accounting view is explicit. Report the denominator, period, cash/expense basis and cost coverage; undefined denominator means no ratio. Do not call currency-denominated net benefit an ROI percentage.

Use a cost ledger for build, integration, software/API, training/adoption, support/operations, human checks and errors. Specify cash versus internal labor/opportunity cost and avoid mixing them without explanation. Show both rollout-only and all-in program views where material. Do not repeat sunk costs in each quarter; retain cumulative totals separately. Keep gross benefit, avoided expenditure, incremental revenue, contribution, net benefit and ROI in separate columns.

Synthetic check: a tool avoids 240,000 annualized costs after verified capture. Build costs 120,000 and annual running costs 60,000. With an even six-month benefit/running-cost pattern, six-month gross benefit is 120,000, costs are 150,000 and net is −30,000. A full-year forecast is +60,000 net; it is not already realized at six months. If the earlier pilot cost 10,000, cumulative all-in six-month net is −40,000 before other foundation costs. Do not silently omit the pilot from program totals.

## Capacity capture and overlap

For hiring avoidance retain evidence of an actual hiring/workload plan, positions not filled, effective dates, fully loaded costs and maintained service. More throughput counts economically when it demonstrably replaces spend or resolves a costly bottleneck. Redeployment requires an owner, destination task, capacity allocation and measured incremental outcome. Reduced expenses require records of actual reductions.

Assign every economic effect a benefit ID and unique population/time window. Allocate recovered capacity among capture paths; allocated hours cannot exceed verified recovered hours. A foundation asset and its downstream tool cannot both own the same saved dollars. If several tools affect one result, use an explicit allocation or portfolio-only claim, not duplicate full claims. An independent residual benefit needs independent evidence.

## Decision discipline

Precommit pass/fail thresholds and quality/service guardrails, not just a savings goal. Record a decision to scale, revise with a specific change and new cap/date, hold for named evidence, or stop. Preserve the original funded target when forecasts change. Review withdrawn and negative results openly. Reconcile budget headroom separately from profitability: financial success does not itself authorize spending.
