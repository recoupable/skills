# Royalty reconciliation

## Answer to the requested question

State which accounting branches completed and which depend on unavailable evidence. Identify payee, right/provider, territory, usage/reporting periods and currencies. Do not combine historical earnings, adjustments and cash as recovery.

## Sources and controls

Link original source inventory, normalized ledger, row journal, source receipts and control results. Show revisions/replacements, missing periods, excluded rows and unavailable or failed controls. Explain amount basis and rounding.

## Reported earnings and entitlement

Show separate currency/period totals for reported earnings and supported calculated entitlement. Link calculation rules to exact agreement/amendment clauses. Identify opening recoupment balances, reserves, fees, withholding, signed adjustments and unsupported terms. Unknown is not zero.

## Explained differences

Present comparable-period/right/provider analysis. Separate scope/timing/revisions, base income, one-time credits, bonuses, reserves/recoupment, FX and unresolved hypotheses. A discontinuity alone is not a collectible debt.

## Remittances and receipts, when requested

Show allocation IDs, relevant remittance and receipt references, amounts/currencies, fees or FX basis, evidence status and residuals. Keep provider-paid status distinct from observed bank-posted cash.

## Exceptions and bounded next steps

Link each exception to source versions, affected objects/periods, basis, controls, supported amount or unknown, and exact evidence/remedy needed. A handoff can request missing accounting without asserting an unsupported debt. No demand or provider contact is implied.

## Reproduction and continuation

Record helper/version, command inputs, result files, private case ID/revision and changed-source dependencies. Preserve a separate old/new-period view for transfer tails. Identify what a later source could resolve.
