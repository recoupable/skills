# Recording-rights societies

Checked 2026-10-22. Preserve owner, featured/non-featured performer and creative
participant roles separately, with territory, period and actual collection mandate.

## SoundExchange

The creator/rightsholder route is SoundExchange Direct. Public ISRC search is an
observation source; Licensee Direct is not the creator claim route. Prepare the exact
recording, eligible role and supported interest before using an authorized account.
Record claim acceptance and later royalty/payment evidence independently.
[Registration and service routes](https://www.soundexchange.com/register/).

A featured-artist Letter of Direction directs part of that artist's allocation to
an eligible creative participant. It is not a general transfer of master ownership
or a payment from the top of all recording income. Prepare the current authentic
form, relevant recordings/effective dates and appropriate directing signatures;
multiple directing artists can require separate support. Do not manufacture signatures
or apply a producer direction to unrelated participants. [LOD guidance](https://www.soundexchange.com/what-we-do/for-artists-labels-and-producers/letters-of-direction/).

## PPL

Recording-rightsholder members add repertoire; performer members claim performances
on existing recordings. A missing recording requires the rightsholder's repertoire
step, not unauthorized owner registration by a performer. Prepare current required
recording/ISRC/performer-lineup data and ownership territory/date scope. Incomplete
data can leave a record invalid. Updates are limited by who submitted the recording;
transfers use the documented member-services process with scope/date/evidence.
Inspect the accepted record rather than treating upload as completion.
[Repertoire procedures](https://www.ppluk.com/royalties/uk-royalties/register-your-repertoire/).

## SENA

MySena distinguishes musician and producer/master-owner roles. A person occupying
both roles needs the appropriate separate registration/account treatment. Preserve
per-recording participation or ownership and the actually granted mandate. A listed
track or reserved gross amount is not a bank receipt. Verify current fields, territorial
rights and authentic user role in the authorized account before filing.
[Music-creators FAQ](https://sena.nl/en/faq-music-creators).

These profiles establish preparation questions and documented routes. None includes
a live account, automated filing adapter, universal worldwide mandate or certified
payment result. Substantive conflicts require their exact provider resolution process;
legal ownership conclusions and cash receipt remain separately evidenced.
