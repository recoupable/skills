# Composition repertoire and provider readiness

Preserve compositions, party identities/aliases, recording links, interests, mandates
and registry observations as separate records. ISWC, IPI, ISNI and provider work/party
IDs are scoped identifiers; missing IDs are allowed. Keep writer and publisher entities
separate even when a person controls the company. A label's name or account login is
not a publishing mandate. Alternative titles are observations attached to a reviewed
work, not a reason to merge namesake works automatically.

An interest says kind (owned/writer/administered/collected/statement percentage), party,
work/right, amount, basis/denominator, territory, term and supporting evidence. An
administration or reciprocal collection edge also has a parent and mandate source.
A repeated downstream administrator under several publishers does not multiply owned
shares. Do not add interests with different bases, rights, scales, periods or parent
chains; retain provider conventions and overlapping/conflicting observations.

Maintain historical affiliation at party/right/territory/date scope. A writer's new
PRO does not automatically move a separate publisher entity, past service periods,
every composition, direct license or another territory's mandate. Post-term collection
can coexist with a successor administering new periods. Record unknown transition dates
and the exact notices/releases required; do not infer termination from a new logo.

For each requested provider/work, record operation (new work, existing-interest claim,
amendment, match, transfer), account role, mandate, right and territory, current official
route, known identifier/match, evidence gaps and readiness conclusion. Use observations:
unchecked; not_found_in_named_source; listed_in_supplied_export; observed_registry_record;
or provider_accepted_with_readback. A missing search result is scoped to the query/date
and coverage. Private or unavailable accounts remain unchecked. A zero membership list
is not a royalty ledger. Registration, record observation and financial outcomes remain
independent. Keep payload preparation/submission out of this organization stage.

Synthetic review scenarios: an unreleased composition with no ISWC remains in inventory;
a performer and writer with the same display name remain distinct parties until matched;
an administrator listed below two publishers remains two sourced collection edges;
a historical performance affiliation does not override a present mechanical mandate.
Return the repertoire, readiness matrix, exact missing documents and relevant source
versions. Do not price a recovery opportunity from any of these gaps.
