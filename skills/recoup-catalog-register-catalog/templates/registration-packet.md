# Registration operation packet

## Requested operation

Case/action/item IDs; represented party and role; exact provider/account; operation; objects; rights, territories and effective dates. State preparation-only or requested submission scope.

## Evidence and identity

Existing-record searches and observed IDs/versions; identity match basis; agreements/mandates/consents; source versions and precise locators. Preserve uncertain matches and authentic-document gaps.

## Provider rules and route

Official source URLs and check date; current operation/schema/version; account eligibility; supported transport; share basis; required fields/documents; validation performed. State any unresearched requirement or unavailable transport.

## Exact payload and attachments

Link the exact before/after fields or provider file, hash, per-item manifest and necessary authentic attachments. For transfers include source/target interests, retained rights, effective dates, release conditions and collection tails.

## Consequences and action authority

State recipients, onward/public disclosure, fees, required human certifications and consequential changes. Cite existing user authority covering this action. List only genuinely missing decisions after the packet is concrete.

## Submission and provider outcome

Capture attempts, receipts, raw item statuses, errors, accepted record IDs, superseding/rematching history and effective dates. Never treat a generated packet as submitted.

## Readback and remaining work

List expected versus observed external fields, observation references/dates, source and target states, blocked items and next owner. Keep acceptance separate from readback and financial effects. Preserve pending/partial results.
