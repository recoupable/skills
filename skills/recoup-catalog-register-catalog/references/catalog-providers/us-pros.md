# U.S. performance societies

Checked 2026-10-22. Capture the writer/publisher affiliation and mandate for the
actual period. Historic and current affiliations are separate evidence; do not
bulk-replace historical records from a current affiliation statement.

## BMI

Distinguish a new complete work from an existing-work update. Keep the provider's
writer/publisher percentage convention explicit: a 200% basis cannot be interpreted
as a simple ownership overclaim. Inspect current eligibility and permissions in the
registration terms. [Registration terms](https://www.bmi.com/legal/work-registration-terms-and-conditions).

For updates prepare current title/work number, available account number, exact
before/after fields and required authentic support. BMI directs corrections through
its update procedure rather than a new registration. Reducing/removing an interest
needs documented support. Administration changes require the documented terms,
work scope and signatures; termination notices preserve effective date, retained
works and post-term collection. Use the verified current contact route, capture
the case response and inspect the updated record. [Work updates](https://www.bmi.com/creators/instructions_for_updating_registered_works).

## ASCAP

The detailed [registration help](https://www.ascap.com/help/registering-your-music)
returned HTTP 403 during this review. This is not evidence that member operations
are unavailable. Prepare the work/party/split/mandate evidence, then inspect current
Member Access instructions or supplied official requirements for the selected new
registration/update. Do not label guessed fields or a BMI-shaped form ASCAP-validated.
Read back actual account/work records after any separately authorized filing.

## SESAC and Global Music Rights

SESAC's public help separates affiliate support from licensee support. Its FAQ
describes invitation-only affiliation; account enrollment is not implied by catalog
review. Use an existing affiliate's supported work/update process with current
instructions. [Help](https://www.sesac.com/help-center/),
[FAQ](https://www.sesac.com/frequently-asked-questions/).

Global Music Rights exposes public catalog and client-portal entry points, but this
review did not verify a field-level registration API or schema. Prepare client/work
evidence and obtain the specific client process; do not use a license-purchase route
to submit repertoire. [Official site](https://globalmusicrights.com/).

## Statements and corrections

Use supplied exact exports and their documented meanings. Keep writer and publisher
payees, performance types, cue/program/episode evidence, credits, premiums, bonuses
and adjustment periods distinct. Financial parsing and claim escalation require
their own tested schema/current evidence. None of these procedural profiles proves
an authenticated statement adapter, correct calculation or cash receipt.
