# Verify a provider operation before using it

This skill provides a provider-neutral preparation/execution workflow. The existence
of this document does not implement a service adapter, grant membership, or prove
that a provider accepts a particular file. Check current official documentation and
the account's actual capabilities for the requested operation before filing.

## Operation profile

Capture these fields in the registration packet:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Provider and operation | Exact new-work, existing-interest, amendment, match, transfer, identifier or copyright process |
| Party and role | Represented legal party; writer/publisher/admin/owner/performer/producer role; relevant provider account |
| Scope and authority | Right, territory, period, mandate/consent and source versions |
| Rules and schema | Official source URL, retrieval date, account-specific instructions, accepted schema/version and required fields |
| Share and identity conventions | Documented share basis and identifiers; records searched and matching evidence |
| Route | Verified API operation, eligible exchange/import, authorized portal procedure or prepared/user-assisted packet |
| Costs and disclosure | Actual known fee, human attestation, documents, recipient/forwarding/publication effects |
| Error and retry behavior | Per-item errors, attempt/receipt identifiers, idempotency support and uncertain-outcome inspection |
| Outcome and readback | Returned acknowledgment/acceptance, effective date, query/export/portal record and expected propagation |
| Capability evidence | Preparation validation, tested execution, or authorized live verification; scope/date of each proof |

An adapter supports only its documented operations. A provider's search endpoint does
not imply write capability. A CWR or DDEX-valid file still needs the recipient's
supported version, onboarding and eligible transport. If the route is unavailable,
prepare the real packet and record the remaining user/provider step.

## Service-specific questions that change the path

These are research/check prompts, not complete filing specifications:

- **US PROs:** establish actual affiliation and represented writer/publisher role for
  the period. Verify the current work registration and correction routes, share basis,
  mandates and supporting signatures. Historical and current affiliations can differ.
- **The MLC:** distinguish public search, songwriter tools and member/account filing
  capabilities. Identify an existing work before claiming an interest or linking a
  recording. Distinguish ordinary claims from overclaim/dispute and transfer processes.
  Administrator/parent relationships do not establish beneficial ownership.
- **Publisher/administrator:** inspect the mandate and actual repertoire/intake route.
  Accepted repertoire and reported accounting are independent. Do not assume public
  write APIs or authorize parallel direct collection that conflicts with the mandate.
- **SoundExchange/recording societies:** distinguish recording-owner, performer,
  featured-artist and producer-payment roles. An owner registration, performance claim
  and authentic direction for producer participation need different authority.
- **International collection:** verify performance/mechanical/recording rights,
  territories, reciprocal representation, local roles and mandates per society.
  A worldwide label on an agreement is not proof of every service's coverage.
- **Copyright office:** separate registration, supplementary correction and transfer
  recordation; verify current subject-matter eligibility, form/deposit, fee and
  human certification. Do not represent filing as legal validity or office acceptance.
- **Identifier issuers:** verify eligible registrant/agency, existing assignment and
  actual identity change. Keep requested and issued identifiers separate.

## Unavailable or unknown service

For a documented provider with no usable submission transport, give a validated
preparation packet only to the extent the current rules/schema were actually checked.
Identify the remaining account-holder action and preserve prepared status.

For an unresearched provider, deliver the user's evidence, proposed operation, known
objects and missing rule/schema questions. Label the packet **not validated for
filing**. Do not create a guessed form, invent an API or claim universal service support.

Before publishing capability claims, retain an authorized live receipt and accepted
record readback for the specific operation/role. Synthetic fixtures establish logic
coverage; they cannot establish provider execution or acceptance.
