# Stylized motion workflow

Use the approved song, scene windows and character sheets from this skill. Choose a provider
that supports the proposed references and intended use. Verify its current model schema,
reference limits, duration, resolution and pricing before proposing a paid run. Do not route
around a provider rejection; revise the concept or report the unsupported requirement.

For each scene, specify the approved reference identities, opening composition, physical
movement, camera movement, timing and closing composition. Bind every image/video reference
to its actual upload order. Keep the visual style consistent across approved stills before
animating. Keep the original music master separate from generated scene audio.

Show the scene prompt and estimated cost for approval. Generate one short demanding test,
inspect character continuity and movement, then apply approved direction to the remaining
scenes. Record inputs, settings, returned costs and asset paths. Review transitions and full
song coverage in the final edit. Missing provider access is a reported limitation, not an
instruction to retrieve credentials from another workspace.
