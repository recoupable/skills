# Objection responses

Templates, not claims about an actual client. Fill amounts and timelines only from verified evidence.

- Think about it: What specific part would you like to think through? What would need to be true to move forward?
- Over budget: Which outcome matters most? Could we narrow scope to fit the authorized budget?
- Exploring options: Which criteria will determine your decision? Compare the same scope and outcomes.
- Build internally: Compare internal capacity, timing and full cost using the prospect's own inputs. Do not invent acceleration or savings.
- Internal buy-in: Identify the decision-maker and prepare a short, evidenced case for the proposed work.
- Bad experiences: Ask what failed, what evidence would rebuild trust, and what a small agreed milestone would prove.
