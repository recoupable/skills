# From evidence to a complete draft

Use this sequence when drafting or revising an article, post, newsletter, email or other public copy.
It complements the house voice and public-content quality gate. It does not authorize publication.

The approach adapts the fragment, shape, beat and editing practices in Matt Pocock's
`mattpocock/skills` writing skills. These instructions are self-contained; those skills are optional,
not runtime dependencies. Follow the user's requested level of collaboration.

## 1. Collect the material before choosing a structure

Read the existing draft and its authorized evidence. Note the specific scene, question, observation,
quotation, example and useful disagreement. Keep attribution, dates and qualifications attached in the private evidence record. Apply the
client-recognition test before selecting details for public copy; an accurate private fact is not
automatically publishable.
Do not invent a personal experience, customer result, number or emotional reaction to improve a hook.

Separate observed results, proposals, current product behavior and illustrative examples. Check changing
claims in primary sources when required. Missing private evidence remains a recorded gap; do not
retrieve excluded originals or turn the absence of a record into a claim that an event never happened.

For a short piece, a few notes are enough. Revisions should preserve strong material, including
deliberate voice and useful awkwardness. A file can receive a reasoned keep decision.

## 2. Define what the reader needs

Write privately: who is reading, what question they bring, what they need to understand first,
the answer this piece can support and the next useful action.

Arrange prerequisites before the points that depend on them. If a report promises to show change,
identify both periods and the records to compare. If a workflow needs existing access, say so before
describing what the request accomplished.

For Recoup business content, identify the agent task that makes the subject useful: input, action,
output, and the human decision or review when relevant. State the evidence status privately before
writing: implemented, established method, proposed or illustrative. Build that connection through
the argument instead of adding an AI sentence at the end.

Use the title and opening to make a promise the body can fulfill. Prefer one worked example that
develops through the explanation to several disconnected examples.

## 3. Choose a shape and test the sequence

Consider the simplest complete structure for this material: a worked example, scene and explanation,
comparison, practical procedure, argument or question and answer. Do not force every article into
an essay template or an identical opening.

List the few changes in understanding the reader needs. Each paragraph or section should add one.
Read headings alone to check progression. Move, merge or remove sections that repeat an earlier job.

Where several structures would materially change the argument, show the user concise alternatives
if they want to collaborate. For an authorized batch, use editorial judgment and record the chosen
shape. Do not interrupt a batch for an approval question about every section.

Stop when the reader has the promised answer and useful next step. Word targets are aids, not a
reason to pad a complete explanation.

## 4. Write each channel as a complete piece

Revise the article first when it is the source. Then inspect every companion independently.
A native social post or email needs enough context, evidence and practical value to stand alone;
it should not merely say that an article exists.

Preserve quotes faithfully. If a long quote harms the reading flow, choose a faithful shorter excerpt
or use a clearly attributed paraphrase. Never split a quotation into misleading fragments.

Use short prose paragraphs, with a target ceiling of 240 visible characters. Count visible link text,
not Markdown URLs. Headings, lists, tables, code and long exact quotations need format-aware judgment.
Shorten or restructure a long sentence; do not mechanically chop it or damage a quote.

Check the title, subject metadata, visible subject, excerpt, hook and CTA together. Use a consistent
term for the same thing. Remove placeholders that require an unavailable article or destination;
preserve intentional email-personalization fields until dispatch resolves them.

## 5. Review with fresh eyes, then reconcile the bundle

Run the required fresh reader and copy-editor passes. Reviewers return notes; the author chooses and
applies edits. Re-check any edit that changes facts, attribution, scope or the implied result.

In a user-authorized batch adaptation, a fresh reviewer may handle a named group. They must read every
file individually before comparing companions and record a keep/fix decision for each. Say that this
was a batch review, not a separate reviewer per file. Do not report a numeric score unless an actual
scoring procedure produced it. Simulated reader feedback is not customer research.

After edits, inspect cover text, actual image pixels, labels, arrows and alt text. A figure must not
promise funding, accuracy, automatic execution or a delivered feature that the copy does not support.
An arrow communicates sequence or cause; two related options may need a comparison instead.

Re-render changed editable figures. Inspect covers at full size and around 160px, and section images
at article width. Image paths alone do not establish that the right artwork is embedded.

## 6. Save a checkable revision record

Keep evidence and review notes private. For each file, record its path, final content hash, disposition,
review method, accepted changes and unresolved evidence or delivery needs. Record the client
recognition finding, conversational-voice finding, agent task and human decision/review rationale
for each file; use an explicit reason where a review point does not apply. Preserve historical review
records as historical; do not carry their pass labels onto revised bytes.

Keep source content in its canonical bundle. Calendars, previews and channel variants should point to
that source or record the exact version they derived from. Refresh any publication fingerprint when
copy, metadata, image bytes, image order or the release slot changes.

Completion means every in-scope file has a disposition, accepted edits are applied, cross-channel
claims agree, required visuals were inspected and actual checks are recorded. Draft review, repository
publication, channel approval, sending and live delivery are separate states.
